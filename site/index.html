How to save landmarks

Save landmarks once. Follow task steps later.

Name the website

A notebook holds landmarks and task steps for one website.

Place landmarks

Choose placement, then use a pointer or arrow keys to mark a visible control.

Follow task steps

Open the guide to read or hear one step at a time. It never clicks for you.

Private by design

Your landmarks stay in your browser.

  • Notebooks are stored in extension local storage.
  • Screenshots are temporary during placement.
  • Your browser may suggest visible text; manual placement always works.
  • Backups are encrypted with your passphrase.
  • No analytics, ads, or remote control.

When to recheck landmarks

Landmarks use screen coordinates. Recheck them after a browser app, display scale, or window layout changes.
