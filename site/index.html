The repeatable method

Observe once. Follow it next time.

Name the app

Make a notebook tied only to the app’s site origin. No page text or credentials are collected.

Place landmarks

After a deliberate click, freeze the visible tab and place pins by pointer or arrow keys. Browser-local OCR helps only when available.

Write and follow

Attach each concise instruction to a pin. The overlay can show or speak one step at a time without clicking for you.

Private by construction

Your map belongs to you.

  • Recipes live in local extension storage.
  • Screenshots are temporary and never uploaded.
  • OCR uses the browser’s on-device detector when available.
  • Exports use AES-256-GCM with your passphrase.
  • No analytics, ads, telemetry, or remote control.

An honest boundary

Coordinate landmarks work best when the remote app’s layout stays stable. They do not create accessibility metadata, automate clicks, read credentials, or promise compatibility with every remote desktop.
