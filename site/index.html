What stays on your device

Your landmarks stay in your browser.

  • Notebooks use storage inside your browser extension.
  • Screenshots are removed when placement closes.
  • Text suggestions stay in placement; manual placement remains available.
  • Backups are encrypted with your passphrase.
  • No analytics, ads, or remote control.

When to recheck landmarks

Landmarks stay at saved screen positions. Recheck them after the app, display scale, or window layout changes.
