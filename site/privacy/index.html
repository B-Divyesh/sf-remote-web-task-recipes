Privacy, in plain language

The extension keeps its notebook on your device. We do not run accounts, analytics, ads, or a remote service.

Data stored on your device

A notebook contains its website address, landmarks, task steps, and preferences. The extension stores this in its local browser storage. Removing the extension may remove it. Export a backup first if you need one.

Screenshot and text suggestions

You choose when to open placement. The screenshot is used only while placement is open. It is not stored in the notebook. Your browser may suggest visible text if it supports that feature. You can place a landmark manually either way.

Encrypted backups

The extension encrypts a backup on your device with a passphrase you choose. Do not lose the passphrase: we cannot recover it.

Analytics and third parties

The extension has no analytics, advertising trackers, runtime CDNs, accounts, checkout requests, or remote API calls. The site host may create ordinary server access logs. We do not sell personal data.

Your control

Delete notebooks in the editor. Remove the extension to clear its storage. Use your browser controls to clear site data. Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
