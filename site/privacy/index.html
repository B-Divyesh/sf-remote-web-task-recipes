Privacy, in plain language

The extension keeps its notebook on your device. We do not run accounts, analytics, ads, or a remote service.

Data stored on your device

A notebook contains its website address, landmarks, task steps, and preferences. The extension stores this in its browser storage.

Removing the extension may remove its data. Export a backup first if you need one.

Screenshots and text suggestions

You choose when to open placement. A screenshot exists only while placement is open. It is not stored in the notebook.

Your browser may suggest visible text in the temporary screenshot. Choose a label to position a landmark, or place it manually.

Encrypted backups

The extension encrypts each backup on your device with your passphrase. We cannot recover a lost passphrase.

Analytics and third parties

The extension has no analytics, advertising trackers, runtime CDNs, accounts, checkout requests, or remote API calls.

The extension does not send notebook data to us.

Your control

Delete notebooks in the editor. Remove the extension to clear its storage. Use browser controls to clear site data.

Questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
