Terms of use

Remote Web Task Recipes is a local accessibility aid for recording user-owned visual landmarks and instructions. By using it, you agree to these terms.

What the tool does

The extension displays coordinate-based landmarks and user-written steps over ordinary browser tabs. It does not control the remote app, create missing accessibility metadata, verify that an action succeeded, or guarantee compatibility with every browser-delivered or remote-desktop system.

Your responsibilities

Do not record passwords, authentication codes, private customer data, or instructions you are not authorized to keep. Confirm important actions in the underlying software. Follow your employer’s applicable policies when creating or sharing a notebook.
